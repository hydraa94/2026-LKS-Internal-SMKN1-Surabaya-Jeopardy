Object

Pillars of Creation

Instrument

James Webb

Observation

Oct 2022

NASA's James Webb Space Telescope captured this extremely detailed landscape where new stars are forming within dense clouds of gas and dust. The three-dimensional pillars look like majestic rock formations but are far more permeable.

Object

Carina Nebula

Instrument

James Webb

Observation

July 2022

This landscape of "mountains" and "valleys" speckled with glittering stars is actually the edge of a nearby, young, star-forming region called NGC 3324 in the Carina Nebula.

Object

Orion Nebula

Instrument

Hubble / Spitzer

Observation

Deep Sky Archive

The Orion Nebula is a picture-book of star formation, from the massive, young stars that are shaping the nebula to the pillars of dense gas that may be the homes of budding stars.

Object

Andromeda Galaxy

Instrument

Hubble

Observation

M31 Survey

Andromeda is the closest large spiral galaxy to our own. Hubble has captured over 100 million stars and thousands of star clusters within a section of the galaxy’s pancake-shaped disk.

Object

Sombrero Galaxy

Instrument

Hubble / Spitzer

Observation

M104

The galaxy's hallmark is a brilliant white, bulbous core encircled by thick dust lanes comprising the spiral structure of the galaxy. It is located 28 million light-years from Earth.

Object

Whirlpool Galaxy

Instrument

Hubble

Observation

M51

The Whirlpool Galaxy is a classic spiral galaxy. Its graceful, curving arms are actually long lanes of stars and gas laced with dust.
